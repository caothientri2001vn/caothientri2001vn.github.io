Biography

Currently, I am a second-year PhD student at the National University of Singapore (NUS), fortunate to be under the joint supervision of Prof. Bryan Hooi and Prof. Shuicheng Yan, with the sponsorship of the AI Singapore PhD Fellowship.
Previously, I earned my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City – University of Science, where I studied in the Advanced Program in Computer Science (APCS) and graduated with Highest Distinction.

Research Interests

My research broadly focuses on trustworthy perception and interaction for AI agents, particularly how agents can reliably and safely understand multimodal inputs and interact with their environments. More recently, I have been exploring the challenges of moving AI agents beyond digital environments and into the physical world, with an emphasis on developing robust perception capabilities for operation in real environments.
